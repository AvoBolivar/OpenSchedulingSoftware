# System Context: Open-Source Business Management System

## System Overview
This repository contains the source code for a comprehensive, open-source general business management system. The primary goal of this application is to provide small and medium-sized business (SMB) owners with a free, accessible, and all-in-one platform to manage their daily operations, finances, and workforce. 

The target audience includes service-based businesses, independent contractors, salons, and retail shops that need a unified dashboard to replace fragmented, costly software stacks.

## Core Modules & Features

### 1. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
*   **Client Profiles:** Store contact details, preferences, and activity history.


### 2. Scheduling & Appointments
*   **Calendar View:** Visual scheduling interface for booking customer appointments and internal meetings.
*   **Automated Reminders:** SMS or email notifications to reduce no-shows.

### 3. Human Resources & Employee Management
*   **Staff Profiles:** Manage employee details, roles, and permissions within the app.
*   **Time / Job Tracking:** Clock-in/clock-out functionality and timesheet management or employees can be attached to a job/customer appointment and they are responsible for the job.
*   **Schedule Management:** Assign shifts and manage employee availability.

### 4. Financial Management
*   **Revenue & Expense Tracking:** Log income streams and categorize operational expenses.
*   **Profit & Loss (P&L) Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into the financial health of the business.

### 5. Invoicing, Quotes & Estimates (Recommended Addition)
*   **Quote Generation:** Allow businesses to draft and send estimates or quotes for project work before approval.
*   **Invoicing:** Convert approved quotes or completed appointments directly into professional invoices.
*   **Payment Tracking:** Monitor pending, overdue, and paid invoices.

### 6. Job & Project Tracking (Recommended Addition)
*   **Project Stages:** Track the lifecycle of a specific job (e.g., Lead -> Quoted -> In Progress -> Completed -> Billed).
*   **Task Assignment:** Break down larger jobs into tasks and assign them to specific employees.

### 7. Inventory & Asset Management (Recommended Addition)
*   **Stock Tracking:** Keep counts of physical goods, materials, or retail products.
*   **Equipment Tracking:** Log and manage the location and maintenance schedules of expensive business tools and assets.

### 8. Analytics & Reporting (Recommended Addition)
*   **Business Intelligence:** Generate reports on top-performing employees, most profitable services, and seasonal trends to drive strategic decision-making.
